# Correspondence — requests as sent, and what came back

This folder holds the correspondence of the Education Records Project: the request letters exactly as sent, and the agencies' acknowledgments, denials, and fee demands as received. A refusal is part of the collection — and of the coverage.

**Layout:** one subfolder per jurisdiction (`<jurisdiction-code>/<request-id>/`), created as that request's correspondence is filed. The letters themselves are added as productions and replies arrive; the project's [Request Register](../register.md) already carries every request, its route, and its status as of October 25, 2026.

**The standard request.** Every formal request under this project is filed by Charles Mayfield, Producer, Education Across the States, under the jurisdiction's own public-records law, and asks for the same core menu, in electronic form where it exists:

1. Check register / disbursement ledger (spreadsheet) — FY2025 full year + FY2026 year-to-date
2. Purchase orders — same periods
3. Agency credit-card / p-card transaction records — same periods
4. Payroll register / salary listing — most recent fiscal year
5. Adopted agency budgets + current budget-versus-actual report
6. Federal grant files: IDEA Part B allocation tables to districts; U.S. Department of Education (OSEP) monitoring reports with the state's responses and corrective action plans; state determination letters (last 5 years)
7. State complaint decisions and due-process hearing decisions, as the agency keeps them (redacted), or the agency's public index of them (last 3 years)
8. Contracts over $50,000 with outside vendors, or the contract register/index (last 3 years)
9. State board of education meeting minutes, or the public link (last 12 months)
10. Agency organizational chart and staff directory (current)
11. The agency's list of record systems / databases, if one is maintained
12. Home-education data the agency holds — notification/registration counts, collection instruments and publication locations, and counts of home-educating students in public funding channels (one jurisdiction's item was re-scoped by addendum where the law changed in 2026; see the Request Register)

Each formal request also asks for a public-interest / news-organization fee waiver (or a cost estimate before charges are incurred, where no waiver applies) and, for records touching student privacy, asks the agency to produce the record **as it keeps it, redacted**, rather than withholding it whole.

Where an agency publishes no verified formal route, the first step is a short custodian-first letter confirming the correct route; the formal request follows. Where the agency takes requests only through its own portal, the filing is made there. No address is ever guessed.
